## Blue Prince Helper
> **_Does it ever end?_**

--- 
This app exists for two main purposes

* I wanted non-googling assistance for an ultra-late game [Blue Prince](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1569580/Blue_Prince/) puzzle
* It gave me a reason to play around with the [WPF framework](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/desktop/wpf/) for the first time

If you have _not_ played [Blue Prince](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1569580/Blue_Prince/), I emphatically implore you to **stop reading** this ReadMe.

Please do not deprive yourself of the ability to play a one in a lifetime puzzle game. Reach out to anyone you know if you feel like your getting stuck on something.

Some of these puzzles you'll encounter are beyond unique in their design.
